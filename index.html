The Western Ghats in Monsoon

Mist, waterfalls and forests turned emerald green.

When to go

The hills are at their greenest from June to September. Waterfalls run full, clouds sit low over the ridges, and the air smells of wet earth.

What to pack

A light rain jacket, shoes with good grip, and a dry bag for your phone and camera.

Good to know

Some trails close during heavy rain. Check local forest department notices before you set out.
